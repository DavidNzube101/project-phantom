## README — How to Use

Included:
- Proposal Template (client-facing, outcome-oriented)
- Master Service Agreement (MSA) — core legal terms
- Statement of Work (SOW) — project-specific scope
- Change Order Form — manage scope changes safely
- Mutual NDA — confidentiality for discovery

How to use:
1) Duplicate the templates and fill in variables ({{like_this}}).
2) Keep commercial terms (fees, timelines) in the SOW; keep legal terms in the MSA.
3) Always use the Change Order form for scope changes.
4) Have a qualified attorney review before first use.

Best practices:
- Focus proposals on outcomes and success metrics.
- Add acceptance criteria to avoid subjective scope creep.
- Cap liability and include a clear change-order process.
- Separate "what" (SOW) from "how/legal" (MSA).
